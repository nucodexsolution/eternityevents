## 3. Services Offered

### A. Weddings & Pre-Wedding Celebrations
* **Complete Wedding Planning:** Turnkey wedding management from concept to teardown.
* **Destination Weddings:** Destination scouting, transit logistics, and hospitality across popular getaways.
* **Themes & Stage Decoration:** Mandap setups, entrance decor, floral arrangements, fairy light canopies, and photobooths.
* **Pre-Wedding Functions:** Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Cocktails, and Ring Ceremonies.
* **Traditional & Cultural Elements:** Band, Baaja & Baraat procession, dhol/tasha troupes, traditional welcomes, and trousseau packing.
* **Styling & Hospitality:** Guest check-in management, welcome hampers, bride & groom styling assistance, and guest coordination.

### B. Private & Milestone Celebrations
* **Birthdays & Anniversaries:** Customized theme decor (balloon arches, LED neon signs, themed backdrops).
* **Baby Welcomes & Baby Showers:** Naming ceremonies (Dohale Jevan / Namkaran), baby arrival setups.
* **Surprise & Romantic Setups:** Candlelight room setups, proposal setups, and terrace dinners.

### C. Corporate & Brand Events
* **Corporate Meets:** Annual general meetings, corporate galas, award nights, conferences, and seminars.
* **Brand Activations:** Product launches, store openings, exhibition stalls, and promotional activations.
* **Celebrity & Artist Management:** Coordinating singers, anchors/emcees, DJs, dance troupes, and celebrity appearances.

### D. Vendor Management & Production Logistics
* **Venue Sourcing & Booking:** Banquet halls, lawns, resorts, and heritage properties.
* **Catering & F&B Coordination:** Multi-cuisine catering, live food counters, and custom bar concepts.
* **Audio-Visual & Technicals:** High-end sound setups, trussing, concert lighting, LED video walls, and generator backups.
* **Photography & Videography:** Candid photographers, cinematic wedding films, drone cinematography, and instant photo booths.
* **Stationery & Gifting:** Designer invites, digital invites/websites, and personalized wedding favors.
